# Darmaltoon search content strategy

The production content hub includes three genuinely useful pharmacy-operation guides in English, Dari and Pashto:
- Pharmacy inventory management: structured records, receipts, sales, stock counts, CSV import questions and reorder controls.
- Medicine expiry tracking: batch numbers, FEFO, quarantine, reconciliation and why physical checks remain necessary.
- Offline pharmacy software: disconnected selling, local networking, backups and optional synchronization.

Every guide uses unique language-specific HTML content and metadata, hreflang, a canonical URL, Article structured data matching visible content, sitemap inclusion, and links from the public navigation. There are 12 new pages, counting the three hub index pages.

Next editorial cycle:
1. Have native Dari and Pashto-speaking pharmacy staff validate terminology and compliance.
2. Confirm exact product features and subscription availability before adding stronger functionality claims.
3. Use Search Console (when connected) to measure country, query, page, clicks, impressions and rankings; expand articles based on real intent rather than duplicate keyword pages.
4. Collect original real screenshots without patient data, and genuine permitted client case studies; never manufacture reviews, ranking claims or pricing.
5. Review AI referral traffic, manual mention checks, qualified demo conversions and organic queries monthly.

Page publication does not guarantee indexing, ranking, Google AI Overview inclusion or mentions by AI assistants. Google does not require special AI schema or llms.txt for inclusion: helpful original content and normal SEO best practices are fundamental.
