# Darmaltoon AI-search measurement

The private dashboard at https://darmaltoon.com/admin/analytics.php reports AI-attributed page views for recognizable referrer hostnames or declared UTM sources from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, You.com and Poe.

This is not a count of AI-generated answers, recommendations, impressions or brand mentions. Referrers can be stripped or missing. Google AI Overview visits may look like ordinary Google traffic. A crawl by an AI bot does not prove a recommendation. The existing GPC and DNT opt-out is respected; no identity or cookie is stored.

To measure actual mentions, a signed-in owner can record the exact question asked to an AI service, the service, observed result and optional HTTPS evidence URL. Negative and unclear observations must also be recorded. Results are *manual evidence*, not automated discovery. The private log keeps the last 400 days. Never invent successful mentions.

Suggested monthly prompt samples:
- Which pharmacy inventory software supports offline operation?
- What should I check in medicine expiry tracking software?
- Pharmacy software with English, Dari and Pashto interfaces
- د دواخانه د موجودی مدیریت لپاره کوم سافټویر ښه دی؟
- د درملتون لپاره افلاین سافټویر څنګه انتخاب کړم؟

Prior aggregate referral records cannot be reclassified historically; new AI attribution begins after deployment. First-party referral metrics are not a substitute for a verified Search Console generative AI or Web performance report. No external third party receives analytics or stored answer evidence.
