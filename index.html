FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Darmaltoon.

Is Darmaltoon suitable for small pharmacies?

Darmaltoon is designed for small pharmacies as well as growing teams. Contact us to discuss which deployment and licensing option is suitable.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

The Windows desktop application supports offline-first workflows. Cloud synchronization and Android offline capabilities depend on the selected configuration and product availability.

Which languages are supported?

Darmaltoon offers English, Dari and Pashto interfaces. Confirm language coverage for your selected product modules.

Is my data secure?

Darmaltoon provides access-control and backup options. The exact backup and synchronization arrangements depend on deployment and licensing.

Can multiple staff members use the system?

Yes. Create staff accounts and give each person only the permissions they need.

How do I get started?

Request a free demo. We will walk you through the system and help you set up your pharmacy.
