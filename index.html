Frequently asked questions

Does Darmaltoon work offline?

The Windows desktop application is designed for offline-first operation. Browser access and synchronization depend on connectivity and configuration.

Is there an online web application?

Yes, Darmaltoon includes a web-based pharmacy management application; module availability depends on setup.

Can staff sell using Android?

An Android sales companion is included in the product roadmap and offering. Contact us to confirm current release availability and supported workflows.

What languages are supported?

English, Dari and Pashto; Dari and Pashto use right-to-left interfaces.

Are the sales numbers real?

No. Dashboard figures in the design previews are clearly illustrative demonstrations.
