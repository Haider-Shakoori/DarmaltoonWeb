FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Darmaltoon?

Darmaltoon is pharmacy-management software designed for pharmacies. It helps manage sales, inventory, purchases, suppliers, customers, returns, expenses, reporting and daily closing.

Who is Darmaltoon designed for?

It is designed for pharmacy owners, managers, pharmacists, growing pharmacies, multi-computer pharmacies and pharmacy chains.

What is PharmaDesktop?

PharmaDesktop is the Windows desktop application used for pharmacy operations within the Darmaltoon system.

Can Darmaltoon be used without reliable internet?

Darmaltoon’s desktop-focused approach is designed with unreliable connectivity in mind. Not every feature is guaranteed to work offline; capabilities depend on configuration.

Can more than one computer use the system?

Darmaltoon supports pharmacy environments using more than one computer where configured.

Which languages are supported?

English, Dari and Pashto.

Does Darmaltoon support inventory management?

Yes. Darmaltoon includes inventory features for tracking medicine and product stock.

Can I manage suppliers and customers?

Yes. Darmaltoon supports managing suppliers, customers and their related transactions.

Does the software include reporting?

Yes. Darmaltoon includes operational and financial reporting features.
