Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about the Darmaltoon platform.

Does Darmaltoon work without internet?

The Windows desktop application is designed for offline-first work. Browser-based functions and synchronization depend on connectivity and the selected configuration.

Can I use Darmaltoon on the web and desktop?

The Darmaltoon product family includes web and Windows desktop experiences. Confirm which modules and deployment options are available for your pharmacy during a demonstration.

What is the Android app for?

The Android companion workflow is intended to simplify selling on the move. Ask our team for current availability and supported functions.

Which languages are supported?

English, Dari and Pashto, with RTL layouts for Dari and Pashto.

How do I request a demonstration?

Use the demo page to review the details required. Live form delivery is still being configured.
