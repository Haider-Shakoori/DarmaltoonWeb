Privacy notice

Privacy & Enquiry Data

A clear explanation of how information submitted through the Darmaltoon website is handled.

Updated 17 October 2026

What information we collect

When you submit a demo or contact request, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, pharmacy or company, phone number, email address, location, preferred language and your message. Some fields are optional.

Why we use this information

We use enquiry details to respond, explain the software, arrange a demonstration and follow up about the question you submitted. We do not show your messages publicly. Please do not enter patient records, medicine prescriptions, payment-card data or other sensitive personal information in marketing forms.

How enquiries are processed

The website sends the submitted details over HTTPS to a server-side intake form. Valid requests are stored in a private server directory outside the public website. Email notification may be attempted so our team can review your request; successful email queueing does not guarantee delivery. To reduce spam, we retain a keyed hash of the visitor IP address for throttling instead of keeping the raw IP address in the lead record.

Retention and deletion

Enquiry records are retained for the time needed to respond and manage related business communications. A fixed automatic deletion period has not yet been established. Contact us if you want a record corrected or deleted; some records may need to be retained where required for operational or legal reasons.

Your choices

Submitting a request is optional. You can reach us directly by email or WhatsApp instead. The site also saves your selected language or light/dark display preference in your browser where that feature is used. If you use WhatsApp or an external email service, that service has its own privacy practices.

Security and limitations

We restrict public access to submitted enquiries and use HTTPS for website requests. No online system provides an absolute guarantee of security. If you believe a submitted record should be updated or removed, contact us.

First-party website analytics

To understand which pages are useful and whether visitors request a demo, Darmaltoon counts page views, interactions with demo/contact links, and the number of requests successfully stored. Aggregated data may include page path, English/Dari/Pashto language, device-width category, broad referring provider and optional campaign label. We do not set analytics cookies or store raw IP addresses, browsing identities, names, messages or pharmacy/patient data in analytics. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable browser analytics collection. Aggregated website metrics are automatically deleted after approximately 13 months. Page views are not the same as unique visitors or search-engine impressions.

Questions about your information?

Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you previously submitted. Include enough details for us to locate your enquiry safely.
