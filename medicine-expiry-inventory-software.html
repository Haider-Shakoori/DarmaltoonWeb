A safe expiry-control workflow for any pharmacy

Enter the correct batch identifier and expiry information when medicine is received. Regularly review near-expiry stock, verify the physical package and investigate inventory discrepancies. First-expiry-first-out (FEFO) is a useful stock-handling principle when clinically and legally appropriate, but do not assume automatic FEFO is included without a demo. Segregate expired, recalled or otherwise unsuitable medicine and follow applicable professional rules for returns and disposal.
